<product_requirements>
The Glenx MedHub is a comprehensive telemedicine app for Ghana, aiming to centralize healthcare access. Users need online consultations, appointments, prescription orders, and diagnostics, with multi-role support and secure data management.

**Implemented Features:**
*   **User Roles:** Patient, Doctor, Hospital, Pharmacy, Ambulance, Herbalist, with dynamic role-specific registration and dashboards.
*   **Core Services:** Doctor search, appointment booking, AI-powered symptom checker, community forum, medical record management, payments, notifications, help & support.
*   **Pharmacy/Ambulance:** Listing screens, product catalog, shopping cart, checkout, ambulance booking with live tracking and distance-based pricing.
*   **Video Consultation:** Integrated via Agora.
*   **Admin Dashboard:** Web-based interface for user and app management.
*   **Promotional Deals:** Service provider advertisement display on patient dashboard.
*   **Hospital Management:** APIs and frontend components for hospital operations.
*   **Technical Stack:** FastAPI backend (MongoDB), Expo/React Native frontend (, Zustand).
